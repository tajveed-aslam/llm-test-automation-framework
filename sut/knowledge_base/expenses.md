# Orbit Labs Expenses Policy (2026)

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense over **$50**. Expenses of $50 or less need only a description.

## Deadlines

Expenses must be submitted within **45 days** of the purchase date.

## Meals

The meal allowance is **$55 per day** for domestic travel and **$85 per day** for international travel.
Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Approvals

Expenses are approved by your line manager. Any single expense over **$1,000** also needs a **Director's** approval.

## Mileage

Business use of a personal car is reimbursed at **$0.62 per mile**.
