# Orbit Labs IT Support (2026)

## Contact

Email the service desk at **support@orbitlabs.example** or call extension **2020**, Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00.

## Response times

- **P1** (critical outage or security incident): response within **30 minutes**, 24/7.
- **P2** (one person blocked): response within **4 hours**.
- **P3** (requests and minor faults): response within **2 business days**.

## Laptops

Laptops are replaced every **48 months**. Report a lost or stolen laptop within **12 hours** so it can be wiped.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least **16 characters**. Multi-factor authentication is required on every company system.
