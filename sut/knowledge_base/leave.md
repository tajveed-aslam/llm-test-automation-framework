# Orbit Labs Leave Policy (2026)

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **24 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Leave must be requested in the HR portal at least **2 weeks** in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## Carry-over

Up to **5 unused days** can be carried into the next year and must be used by **31 March**.

## Sick leave

Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is needed after **3 consecutive days**.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive **18 weeks** of fully paid leave. Other parents receive **4 weeks** of fully paid leave.

## Observance leave

Employees may take **2 days** of paid observance leave per year for religious or cultural observances that are not
public holidays.
