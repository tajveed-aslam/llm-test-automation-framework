# Orbit Labs Remote Work Policy (2026)

## Hybrid working

Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**. The other days are spent in the office.

## Core hours

Everyone must be available between **11:00 and 16:00** local time.

## Home office

Remote workers receive a **one-time $400 home office stipend** and an internet allowance of **$35 per month**.
