# Orbit Labs Leave Policy (2024) — ARCHIVED

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

## Carry-over

Up to **10 unused days** can be carried into the next year.

## Sick leave

Employees receive **8 days** of paid sick leave per year.
